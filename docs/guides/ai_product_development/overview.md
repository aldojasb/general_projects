# Product Development: Some Lessons I've Learned

Throughout my career, I’ve had the chance to work on a variety of ML and AI projects. Some went smoothly, others did not, but each one left me with lessons that shaped how I approach product development. These are not universal rules, just patterns I’ve noticed that might be useful for others working in this space.

## What I’ve Found Helpful

**[How I Start ML Projects: With the Business Problem](../framework_that_embraces_uncertainty/)**
*I’ve seen projects succeed more often when the focus begins with the business challenge, not the technology.*

**[The Skateboard Mindset](../skateboard_mindset/)**
*I’ve learned the value of releasing small, simple versions first and building up from there.*

**[The MoSCoW Method](../the_moscow_method/)**
*Clear prioritization has been a lifesaver when juggling competing demands and limited time*
